Gmail Codex Bridge

A personal, locally operated tool that brings selected Gmail conversations into Codex for translation, summaries, and follow-up review. It is operated by Roman Shostak for his own use and is not a public email service.

How it works

The bridge checks a designated Gmail queue label. It reads queued messages and relevant conversation context, processes supported attachments when needed, and creates or continues the corresponding Codex conversation. After successful processing, it updates the bridge queue and project labels. It does not automatically send email replies.

Google account access

The bridge uses Google OAuth with the Gmail modify permission (gmail.modify) to read selected conversations and manage its queue labels and markers. Access is authorized by the account owner and can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Privacy

Selected email content is provided to Codex for the requested analysis. Local state, reports, and downloaded attachments support processing and continuity. This public website contains information about the tool only; it does not contain mailbox content, OAuth credentials, or access tokens.
